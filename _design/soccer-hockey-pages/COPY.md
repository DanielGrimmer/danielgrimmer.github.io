## 6a — sandbox

- chrome right: Room GreenField · shared dials
- h1: Under the hood
- sub: Both accounts of the same game, side by side, with every dial exposed.
- header label: talking allowed now
- body: Every dial is shared, and either of you may push the ball. Change the
  width, the height, or the duality number — which has to share no factor with
  the width, or the two views stop being views of one game. Then switch a single
  move on in one palette and watch a different move light up in the other: that
  is the duality, with the game taken away from it.
- claims: One across for you is **four across** for them. / A row is a row for
  both of you — that is why you can agree who won. / Set the duality to a factor
  of the width and the whole thing collapses.
- dials: Width / Height / Duality number
- dial note: 4 × 3 = 12 ≡ 1 (mod 11) — one player's three across is the other's
  four.

- board heads: Soccer field / Player 1 · Soccer — Hockey rink / Player 2 · Hockey
- palette title: the moves, switchable
