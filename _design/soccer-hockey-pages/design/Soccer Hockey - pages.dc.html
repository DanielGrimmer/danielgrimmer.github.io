Under the hood

Both accounts of the same game, side by side, with every dial exposed.

Every dial is shared, and either of you may push the ball. Change the width, the height, or the duality number — which has to share no factor with the width, or the two views stop being views of one game. Then switch a single move on in one palette and watch a different move light up in the other: that is the duality, with the game taken away from it.

  • One across for you is four across for them.
  • A row is a row for both of you — that is why you can agree who won.
  • Set the duality to a factor of the width and the whole thing collapses.

The Soccer-Hockey Duality Game

Soccer and Hockey are very different games… or aren't they?

This game is played with a friend, and there is a mystery at its heart: two flatly contradictory ways of seeing one game, both of them right. You are meant to solve it together, afterwards.

The firewall

Separate devices. No peeking at each other's screen. No talking — mute the call. For one game, each of you attends only to your own board. Break the firewall and there is nothing left to reveal.

The rules, in one place

The ball is the orange one. Gold squares are where it may go. A ✕ marks a square already used, closed for the rest of the game. The dots in front of each goal are the only squares that can reach it next move. The board wraps left to right. Player 1 scores at the top right, Player 2 at the bottom left. Nowhere legal to go is a draw.

It's Pac-Man's world, and we're just living in it

The left-most and right-most columns are the same edge. Walk the ball sideways until the star crosses the seam, then step across it: the board is a cylinder, and what leaves one side arrives on the other.
