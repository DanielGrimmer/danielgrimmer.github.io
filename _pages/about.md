I work on the philosophy of physics, with a focus on the metaphysics of space and time.

My current research asks how much of spacetime structure is really doing physical work, and how much is an artefact of how we choose to represent the world. Much of it develops the **ISE method of topological redescription**, a set of tools for removing and replacing a theory's topological underpinnings as freely as we switch between coordinate systems. If topology can be swapped out that easily, then the spacetime manifolds appearing in our theories start to look less like fundamental substances or relations and more like features of our representations — a broadly Kantian view that parallels the dynamics-first view of geometry and the Humean view of laws.

Before turning to philosophy, I worked in physics on quantum information and relativistic quantum information.
