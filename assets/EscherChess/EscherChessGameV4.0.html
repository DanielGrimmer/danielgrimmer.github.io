What is going on with my opponent’s pieces?!?

Part of the fun of the game is to figure this out for yourself. But if you need it, here are some hints about each piece:

Regarding Knights:

Their knights seem to move like bishops. Recall that in this game bishops are short ranged and jumpy.

Regarding Bishops:

Their bishops seem to move like knights.

Regarding Rooks:

Their rooks are actually behaving completely normally (that is, for Escher Chess). Short-ranged and jumpy.

Regarding Pawns:

While their pawns are moving forward normally, they do not seem to capture normally. Concretely, they seem to be attacking forward and two spaces to either side instead of just one. That would also help explain their strange pawn structure...

Regarding King:

Their king moves forward and backwards normally, but whenever it moves sideways (or diagonally) its horizontal movement is doubled. This makes it quite a puzzle how to get a checkmate...
