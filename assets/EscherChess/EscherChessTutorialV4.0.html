The rules, in one place

Ordinary chess, on a board five files wide and ten ranks deep, with four changes. All movement is jumpy: every piece goes straight to its destination and nothing can be blocked, including a pawn's opening double step. Everything is short range: a rook reaches four squares along a rank or a file, a bishop two along a diagonal. The board is a cylinder, so the leftmost and rightmost files are neighbours. And every pawn may open with a double step, from wherever it starts. There is no queen on this board, no castling and no en passant; a pawn reaching the far rank promotes. Checkmate wins; a player with no legal move who is not in check draws.
