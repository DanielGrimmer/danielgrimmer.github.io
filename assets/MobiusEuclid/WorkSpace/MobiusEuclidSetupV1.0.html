Welcome to the Mobius Euclid Game! This is a two-player cooperative puzzle game which should take less than 30 minutes to play.

There is a mystery at the heart of this game which the two players should try to solve after playing their first game together. In brief, there are two seemingly contradictory ways of looking at this game which are nonetheless somehow coherent with each other.

Before getting to the real game, however, you should call a friend and work through this setup page together. For the game to work, it will be important that you and your friend cannot see each other's screens. You can both open the link to this webpage separately on your own devices and the game state will automatically be synchronized across the two browser sessions once you are in the same game room.

Once, you are in the same game room, you and your friend should decide who will play as Mobius and who will play as Euclid. Click the "Change Player Type" button if needed.
