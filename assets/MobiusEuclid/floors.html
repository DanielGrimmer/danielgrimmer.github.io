Parallax · Floor Studio

Paint or stamp text on either floor and the other floor updates live through the Radon transform: a mark on Euclid's floor becomes a sinusoidal band on Möbius's (the sinogram); a mark on Möbius's floor becomes a sheaf of lines on Euclid's (the back-projection). Grid and fibers are chart decorations and are not transformed.
(pick Text, then click a floor)

Möbius's floor — the strip, three tiles shown; alternate tiles are the same points seen after a half turn, so they appear flipped. R*g(x,y) of what you paint here appears on the left.
