The rules, in one place

Yellow squares are where the ball may go. A black O marks a square it has already visited, which it can never use again. The dots in front of each goal are the squares that can reach it next turn. The board wraps left to right but not top to bottom. A player who cannot move loses nothing — the game is simply drawn.
