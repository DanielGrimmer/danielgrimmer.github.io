The rules, in one place

The ball is the orange circle. The yellow highlighted squares are where it can go — short steps to the squares around it, longer passes further out. A ✕ marks a square the ball has already used, which is closed for the rest of the game. The dots in front of each goal are the squares that can reach it on the next move. The board wraps onto itself from left to right (like in Pac-Man). Player 1 scores at the top right, Player 2 at the bottom left. If the player to move has nowhere legal to go, the game is a draw.
